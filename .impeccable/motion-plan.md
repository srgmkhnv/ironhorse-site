# Motion revision — requested after first-version rejection

The user found the first version weak and specifically reported no meaningful motion, especially mobile. Preserve brand/content/contact flow while making motion perceptible on normal mobile devices.

Focal moment: the Light Tunnel becomes a sticky scroll-directed inspection pass. A visible beam scans the paint, camera framing moves from wide to detail, and the headline makes room for the automobile. Touch and a keyboard-operable light control move the same photographic light. A finite entrance shows the mechanism before scrolling; no endless hero autoplay.
Continuity: clip-revealed service imagery, cancellable crossfades for service changes, a full-height compact navigation transition, and animated FAQ expansion. A scroll progress line and moving brand band respond to distance rather than loop.
Feedback: care selections compress/invert; selected-service count updates; branch and service panels use interruption-safe transitions.
Budget: native CSS/WAAPI, one coalesced rAF on scroll/input, cached section geometry refreshed on resize/images/fonts, no canvas/WebGL/framework dependencies, no full-document blur. No rAF when idle/hidden. Continuous movement depends on user scrolling. A persistent motion control disables animation, system reduced motion defaults to off, and explicit user opt-in can restore it.
Verification: real no-preference and reduced-motion mobile contexts, touch input, scroll transforms, elapsed-time frames/video, functional regression, keyboard, resize and fast repeated state changes. Separate visual/motion and technical reviewers after implementation; correct material findings before deployment.
